YouTube Shorts vs Long Videos: 586 Channels That Post Both

0.30×

the views of a long video, for a typical short

By Elijah Westbrook · 2026-10-20 · 2 min read

The short answer

On 586 channels that post both, the typical short got about 0.30× the views of the same channel’s typical long video. Shorts beat long videos on only 22% of channels. And a short paid about 16 cents per 1,000 views, versus $6.19 for long videos.

01

Shorts didn’t even win on views

Most of these channels make long videos first and post shorts too, so this doesn’t prove shorts can’t work. Plenty of channels grow on shorts alone. But on channels doing both, shorts usually lost.

What to do

Don’t assume a short is an easier view than a long video on your channel.

02

And they pay a fraction

At the median, a short needs about 39× the views of a long video to earn the same.

What to do

Use shorts to test ideas fast. When a short takes off, that promise deserves a long video.

03

It’s the most asked question creators have

In 23,660 creator questions on Reddit, Shorts vs long form was the top specific topic: 2,205 questions, more than subscribers, views or money.

What to do

If you’re confused about shorts, you’re in good company. Use them for testing, not for paying the bills.

How I did it

586 channels with at least 3 shorts and 3 long videos (14+ days old) in their public feeds. For each channel we compared the median short with the median long video. Pay numbers come from the RPM study.

The limits

Most of these channels are long-video channels that also post shorts. Shorts-first channels may see the opposite.

Questions

Do YouTube Shorts help small channels grow?

On channels that post both, shorts usually got fewer views than long videos (0.30× at the median). They’re best used to test which ideas people respond to.

How much do YouTube Shorts pay?

Creators who shared their Shorts RPM had a median of about $0.16 per 1,000 views.
