How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the best youTube research tools?

By our score, vidIQ (88/100), TubeBuddy (82/100), 1of10 (73/100). The score counts the features that matter for this kind of tool, the price, the free plan and how long the tool has been around.

What are the most used youTube research tools?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were vidIQ (31 videos), Social Blade (4 videos), TubeBuddy (2 videos), TubeLab (2 videos). We show this but don’t score it, since tools can pay creators to feature them.

What is the cheapest youTube research tool?

Of the tools we priced, Social Blade was the cheapest paid option at $5 a month (Bronze) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free youTube research tools?

Yes. vidIQ: Limited tools and 150 AI credits a month; TubeBuddy: Free plan with basic keyword, title and SEO tools; 1of10: 60 AI credits a month, top 5 outlier results; Social Blade: Public stats pages are free.

How does the score work?

Each tool gets up to 100 points: keyword research 15, outlier videos 15, ai titles / ideas 10, browser extension 10, price 25, free plan 15, years on the market 10. A feature we couldn’t confirm counts as half. Price points go to the cheapest tool and shrink as the price goes up. How often a tool shows up in videos isn’t scored, because tools can sponsor videos. Prices and features come from each tool’s own site, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
