Email marketing and automation tools, compared

Top score: Omnisend, 99/100. Cheapest paid plan: ActiveCampaign, $15/mo. Prices checked 2026-11-07.

The short answer

Newsletters, drip flows and store emails. We compared 9 email marketing tools that came up in our study of 1,787 AI automation videos. Omnisend scored highest (99/100) on features, price, free plan and track record. Omnisend showed up in the most videos (11), but we don’t score that. The cheapest paid plan was ActiveCampaign at $15 a month. 6 have a free plan or are free.

How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the best email marketing tools?

By our score, Omnisend (99/100), Mailchimp (96/100), Klaviyo (96/100). The score counts the features that matter for this kind of tool, the price, the free plan and how long the tool has been around.

What are the most used email marketing tools?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Omnisend (11 videos), Mailchimp (8 videos), Klaviyo (7 videos), ActiveCampaign (7 videos). We show this but don’t score it, since tools can pay creators to feature them.

What is the cheapest email marketing tool?

Of the tools we priced, ActiveCampaign was the cheapest paid option at $15 a month (Starter (1,000 contacts)) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free email marketing tools?

Yes. Omnisend: 250 contacts, 500 emails a month; Mailchimp: 250 contacts, 500 emails a month (250 a day), no automation flows; Klaviyo: 250 active profiles, 500 emails a month, $5 of SMS credit; Resend: 3,000 emails a month (100 a day) or 1,000 marketing contacts; Kit: Up to 10,000 subscribers, 1 user, no visual automations or sequences.

How does the score work?

Each tool gets up to 100 points: automated flows 18, sms 8, ai writing 8, store integrations 12, price 30, free plan 14, years on the market 10. A feature we couldn’t confirm counts as half. Price points go to the cheapest tool and shrink as the price goes up. How often a tool shows up in videos isn’t scored, because tools can sponsor videos. Prices and features come from each tool’s own site, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
