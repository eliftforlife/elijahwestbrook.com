The short answer

Where your AI bots and alerts talk to you and your customers. We compared 6 team chat and messaging apps for bots that came up in our study of 1,787 AI automation videos. Telegram scored highest (100/100) on features, price, free plan and track record. Slack showed up in the most videos (77), but we don’t score that. The cheapest paid plan was Microsoft Teams at $4 a month. 4 have a free plan or are free.

How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the best team chat and messaging apps for bots?

By our score, Telegram (100/100), Discord (79/100), WhatsApp Business (78/100). The score counts the features that matter for this kind of tool, the price, the free plan and how long the tool has been around.

What are the most used team chat and messaging apps for bots?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Slack (77 videos), Telegram (42 videos), WhatsApp Business (21 videos), Microsoft Teams (11 videos). We show this but don’t score it, since tools can pay creators to feature them.

What is the cheapest team chat and messaging apps for bot?

Of the tools we priced, Microsoft Teams was the cheapest paid option at $4 a month (Teams Essentials) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free team chat and messaging apps for bots?

Yes. Telegram: Everything free; about 30 bulk messages a second; Discord: Discord itself is free; Nitro is optional; WhatsApp Business: Business app free; replies to customers free for now; Slack: 90 days of message history, up to 10 apps.

How does the score work?

Each tool gets up to 100 points: bots allowed 15, free bot api 15, talk to customers 12, team channels 10, price 25, free plan 15, years on the market 8. A feature we couldn’t confirm counts as half. Price points go to the cheapest tool and shrink as the price goes up. How often a tool shows up in videos isn’t scored, because tools can sponsor videos. Prices and features come from each tool’s own site, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
