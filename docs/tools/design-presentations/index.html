Top score: Canva, 84/100. Cheapest paid plan: Excalidraw, $6/mo. Prices checked 2026-11-07.

The short answer

Graphics, slides and brand kits, with AI to start the draft. We compared 8 design and presentation tools that came up in our study of 1,787 AI automation videos. Canva scored highest (84/100) on features, price, free plan and track record. Canva showed up in the most videos (71), but we don’t score that. The cheapest paid plan was Excalidraw at $6 a month. 5 have a free plan or are free.

How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the best design and presentation tools?

By our score, Canva (84/100), Figma (84/100), Excalidraw (83/100). The score counts the features that matter for this kind of tool, the price, the free plan and how long the tool has been around.

What are the most used design and presentation tools?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Canva (71 videos), Gamma (21 videos), Adobe Photoshop (15 videos), Figma (5 videos). We show this but don’t score it, since tools can pay creators to feature them.

What is the cheapest design and presentation tool?

Of the tools we priced, Excalidraw was the cheapest paid option at $6 a month (Excalidraw+) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free design and presentation tools?

Yes. Canva: Not stated in sources read; Figma: Starter: unlimited drafts, up to 500 AI credits a month; Excalidraw: Full editor, unlimited collaborators, 10 AI requests a day; Miro: Unlimited members, 3 editable boards, 10 AI credits a month; Pomelli: Whole tool is free during beta.

How does the score work?

Each tool gets up to 100 points: templates 12, ai first draft 12, brand kit 10, team editing 8, price 25, free plan 18, years on the market 15. A feature we couldn’t confirm counts as half. Price points go to the cheapest tool and shrink as the price goes up. How often a tool shows up in videos isn’t scored, because tools can sponsor videos. Prices and features come from each tool’s own site, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
