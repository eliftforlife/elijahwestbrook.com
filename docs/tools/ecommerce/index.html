Store builders and dropshipping tools, compared

Most used in the videos: Shopify (75 of 1,787). Cheapest paid plan: Shopify, $39/mo. Prices checked 2026-11-07.

The short answer

Build a store and automate the product, order and supplier side. We compared 8 store builders and dropshipping tools that came up in our study of 1,787 AI automation videos. Shopify was used in the most videos (75). The cheapest paid plan was Shopify at $39 a month. 6 have a free plan or are free.

How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the most used store builders and dropshipping tools?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Shopify (75 videos), BuildYourStore.ai (12 videos), AutoDS (12 videos), Zendrop (8 videos).

What is the cheapest store builders and dropshipping tool?

Of the tools we priced, Shopify was the cheapest paid option at $39 a month (Basic) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free store builders and dropshipping tools?

Yes. BuildYourStore.ai: Store build is free; you pay Shopify separately; Zendrop: Limited access; no automated fulfillment; Etsy: No monthly fee; you pay per listing and per sale; TikTok Shop: No setup fee found; you pay a cut of each sale; Printify: 5 stores, unlimited designs.

How did you rank them?

By how many of the 1,787 AI automation videos in our study used each tool, then by price. Prices and features come from each tool’s own pricing page and docs, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
