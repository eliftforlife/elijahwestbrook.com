How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the most used AI app and website builders?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Lovable (25 videos), WordPress (20 videos), Base44 (11 videos), Replit (10 videos).

What is the cheapest AI app and website builder?

Of the tools we priced, Framer was the cheapest paid option at $10 a month (Basic) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free AI app and website builders?

Yes. Lovable: 5 build credits a day, up to 30 a month; 20 Cloud credits a month; WordPress: WordPress.com subdomain, 1 GB storage, no plugins, no custom domain; Base44: 25 message credits and 100 integration credits a month, up to 5 apps; Replit: Starter: limited daily Agent use, one published app that expires after 30 days; Framer: Framer domain, 1 GB bandwidth, 1,000 pages, 500 AI credits to try.

How did you rank them?

By how many of the 1,787 AI automation videos in our study used each tool, then by price. Prices and features come from each tool’s own pricing page and docs, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
