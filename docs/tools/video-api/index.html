Video APIs for automations, compared

Most used in the videos: Remotion (21 of 1,787). Cheapest paid plan: Shotstack, $39/mo. Prices checked 2026-11-07.

The short answer

Make videos from code or an n8n flow, no editor needed. We compared 6 video APIs for automations that came up in our study of 1,787 AI automation videos. Remotion was used in the most videos (21). The cheapest paid plan was Shotstack at $39 a month. 6 have a free plan or are free.

How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the most used video APIs for automations?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Remotion (21 videos), FFmpeg (20 videos), HyperFrames (18 videos), Creatomate (3 videos).

What is the cheapest video APIs for automation?

Of the tools we priced, Shotstack was the cheapest paid option at $39 a month (Starter) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free video APIs for automations?

Yes. Remotion: Free for individuals and for-profit companies up to 3 employees; FFmpeg: Whole tool is free; HyperFrames: Whole framework is free; Creatomate: 50 credits, no time limit, renders capped at 480p; JSON2Video: 600 credits, watermarked videos.
