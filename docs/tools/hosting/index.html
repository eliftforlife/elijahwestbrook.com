Hosting for n8n and AI apps, compared

Most used in the videos: Hostinger VPS (46 of 1,787). Cheapest paid plan: DigitalOcean, $4/mo. Prices checked 2026-11-07.

The short answer

Where self-hosted automations and AI-built apps run. We compared 10 hosting for n8n and AI apps that came up in our study of 1,787 AI automation videos. Hostinger VPS was used in the most videos (46). The cheapest paid plan was DigitalOcean at $4 a month. 9 have a free plan or are free.

How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

What are the most used hosting for n8n and AI apps?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Hostinger VPS (46 videos), Google Cloud (21 videos), Vercel (20 videos), Railway (10 videos).
