How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the best AI agents that use a computer?

By our score, Manus (78/100), OpenClaw (73/100), Hermes Agent (73/100). The score counts the features that matter for this kind of tool, the price, the free plan and how long the tool has been around.

What are the most used AI agents that use a computer?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Claude Cowork (55 videos), OpenClaw (20 videos), Hermes Agent (17 videos), Manus (15 videos). We show this but don’t score it, since tools can pay creators to feature them.

What is the cheapest AI agents that use a computer?

Of the tools we priced, Manus was the cheapest paid option at $20 a month (Pro) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free AI agents that use a computer?

Yes. Manus: 300 credits a day (1,500 a month cap), 1 task at a time, 2 scheduled automations; OpenClaw: Whole tool is free; you pay your AI model provider; Hermes Agent: Free software; model provider and hosted services billed separately; Perplexity Comet: Browser is free; no Comet Assistant queries without a paid plan; Genspark: Weekly free quota for standard-mode tasks; size not stated.

How does the score work?

Each tool gets up to 100 points: uses a browser 17, uses your desktop apps 13, runs on a schedule 13, runs on your own computer 7, open source 6, price 28, free plan 11, years on the market 6. A feature we couldn’t confirm counts as half. Price points go to the cheapest tool and shrink as the price goes up. How often a tool shows up in videos isn’t scored, because tools can sponsor videos. Prices and features come from each tool’s own site, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
