The short answer

We compared 25 social media schedulers on what they cost a small business, what extra accounts cost, and which platforms they really post to. 16 cover all five main platforms. Publer is the cheapest of those at $20 a month billed monthly. If you post through code or an AI agent, Outstand is $19 a month for unlimited accounts (we use it ourselves).

How we checked

Every price and platform comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date shown in its row. Where a tool only shows yearly billing, we use its monthly equivalent and say so in the details. We don’t take money to rank tools, and a tool that asks to be added is checked the same way as every other.

The limits

Prices change often, and some tools price by "social sets" or users instead of accounts, so check the pricing page before you buy. Some tools post to TikTok or Instagram through a phone reminder instead of publishing on their own; the details say where.

Questions

What is the cheapest social media scheduler?

For one business posting to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook and LinkedIn, the cheapest tool that covers all five was Publer at $20 a month when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Is there a free social media scheduler?

Yes, with limits. Planoly: Mobile app only, 10 uploads a month; Publer: 3 accounts (not X), 10 scheduled posts per account; Buffer: 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts per channel; Metricool: 1 brand, 20 scheduled posts a month, no LinkedIn or X; Pallyy: 1 social set, 15 posts a month, images only; Post Bridge: 5 posts a month (each platform counts as one); Repurpose.io: 10 videos free; Typefully: Free tier, limits not shown; Tailwind: Free start, limits not shown. Meta Business Suite, YouTube Studio and TikTok also schedule their own platform for free.

Which schedulers post to TikTok and YouTube Shorts automatically?

Planoly, OneUp, Later, Outstand, Publer, Buffer, Metricool, Pallyy, ContentStudio, SocialBee, Blotato, Sendible, Post Bridge, Loomly, Agorapulse, Vista Social, Zoho Social, Repurpose.io. Check each tool’s details: some send a phone reminder for certain posts instead of publishing on their own.

How did you rank them?

Tools that post to all five main platforms come first, then by the lowest monthly price for one business on those five accounts, counting any extra-account fees. Ties go to the tool that covers more platforms.
