How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the best customer support helpdesks?

By our score, Freshdesk (94/100), Help Scout (87/100), Intercom (84/100). The score counts the features that matter for this kind of tool, the price, the free plan and how long the tool has been around.

What are the most used customer support helpdesks?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Zendesk (7 videos), Freshdesk (3 videos), Intercom (3 videos), Gorgias (2 videos). We show this but don’t score it, since tools can pay creators to feature them.

What is the cheapest customer support helpdesk?

Of the tools we priced, Freshdesk was the cheapest paid option at $19 a month (Growth) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free customer support helpdesks?

How does the score work?

Each tool gets up to 100 points: ai answers tickets 15, email 10, live chat 12, shopify app 8, price 30, free plan 10, years on the market 15. A feature we couldn’t confirm counts as half. Price points go to the cheapest tool and shrink as the price goes up. How often a tool shows up in videos isn’t scored, because tools can sponsor videos.
