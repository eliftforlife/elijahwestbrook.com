Top score: Freepik, 80/100. Cheapest paid plan: Freepik, $14.50/mo. Prices checked 2026-11-07.

The short answer

Make images, thumbnails, product shots and ads from a prompt. We compared 12 AI image generators that came up in our study of 1,787 AI automation videos. Freepik scored highest (80/100) on features, price, free plan and track record. Nano Banana (Gemini) showed up in the most videos (70), but we don’t score that. The cheapest paid plan was Freepik at $14.50 a month. 4 have a free plan or are free.

How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the best AI image generators?

By our score, Freepik (80/100), Adobe Firefly (79/100), Magnific (74/100). The score counts the features that matter for this kind of tool, the price, the free plan and how long the tool has been around.

What are the most used AI image generators?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Nano Banana (Gemini) (70 videos), GPT Image (ChatGPT) (41 videos), Midjourney (22 videos), OpenArt (11 videos). We show this but don’t score it, since tools can pay creators to feature them.

What is the cheapest AI image generator?

Of the tools we priced, Freepik was the cheapest paid option at $14.50 a month (Premium) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free AI image generators?

Yes. Adobe Firefly: Free daily generations; limits not stated; Nano Banana (Gemini): Basic image generation and editing in the Gemini app; limits refresh every 5 hours; Leonardo: 150 fast tokens a day; Ideogram: Weekly free credits when signed in with Google, Apple or Microsoft; images are public.

How does the score work?

Each tool gets up to 100 points: readable text in images 12, edit your own photos 12, has an api 8, commercial use 15, price 28, free plan 15, years on the market 10. A feature we couldn’t confirm counts as half. Price points go to the cheapest tool and shrink as the price goes up. How often a tool shows up in videos isn’t scored, because tools can sponsor videos. Prices and features come from each tool’s own site, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
