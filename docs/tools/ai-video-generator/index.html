How we checked

The tools come from our study: we labeled every tool named in 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation and grouped them by what they do. Every price and feature comes from the tool’s own pricing page or help docs, read on the date in its row. Where we couldn’t confirm something, it shows as a question mark instead of a guess. We don’t take money to rank tools.

The limits

Prices change often, and many AI tools charge in credits that run out at different speeds, so check the pricing page before you buy. "Videos" counts how often a tool showed up in tutorials, not how good it is.

Questions

What are the best AI video generators?

By our score, Runway (91/100), Google Veo / Flow (90/100), Fliki (79/100). The score counts the features that matter for this kind of tool, the price, the free plan and how long the tool has been around.

What are the most used AI video generators?

In 1,787 YouTube videos about AI automation, the most used were Higgsfield (80 videos), Seedance (37 videos), Google Veo / Flow (26 videos), Kling (23 videos). We show this but don’t score it, since tools can pay creators to feature them.

What is the cheapest AI video generator?

Of the tools we priced, Runway was the cheapest paid option at $15 a month (Standard) when we checked on 2026-11-07.

Are there free AI video generators?

Yes. Runway: 125 credits once, no refill, watermark; Google Veo / Flow: 50 Flow credits a day; Fliki: About 3 minutes of 720p video a month, watermark; Luma: Images and draft-resolution video only; Higgsfield: No monthly credits; a few models, no commercial use.

How does the score work?

Each tool gets up to 100 points: text to video 12, image to video 15, sound in the clip 15, has an api 8, price 30, free plan 12, years on the market 8. A feature we couldn’t confirm counts as half. Price points go to the cheapest tool and shrink as the price goes up. How often a tool shows up in videos isn’t scored, because tools can sponsor videos. Prices and features come from each tool’s own site, read on the date in its row. Nobody paid to be listed.
